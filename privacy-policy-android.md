# MuStat for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy covers the MuStat app for Android (app ID com.mustat.jj). MuStat for Windows (including its Chrome extension) has its own policy. The app is made by MuStat ("we", "us"). Contact: delucamana@gmail.com.

## The short version
MuStat keeps your listening history on your phone. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We never receive your data.

## What the app reads
To count your listening, MuStat asks for **Notification access**. It uses it only to see what the music players you allow (YouTube Music and Spotify) are playing: song title, artist, album, song length and whether it is playing. It does **not** read the text of your notifications or your messages. Spotify ads and podcasts are ignored.

You can also import a history file you downloaded yourself (Google Takeout for YouTube Music, or Spotify's privacy data download). The app reads the file only on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where
Your listening history, settings, lists (blacklist, custom names) and a small troubleshooting log are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. The log can contain song titles of ads, podcasts or plays that were dropped. The app turns Android's backup off, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

## When data leaves your phone
Only when you choose to:
- **Sync with your own devices.** After you pair devices with a one-time code, they exchange your history directly over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot. The connection is encrypted. It does not go through the internet or through us.
- **Export a copy.** Saves a file where you choose. You can protect it with a password.
- **Share or save a picture** of your stats. The picture goes to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, or to your Photos.
- **Share the troubleshooting log.** Only if you press the button; it goes to the app you pick.
- **Links** to Google Takeout or Spotify open in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

The app's only network use is the local sync above. It makes no requests to the internet by itself.

## What we do not collect
No name, email, account, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, device identifiers or usage analytics. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have it.

## Deleting your data
- In the app: Settings → Reset listening history (this device, or all your paired devices), and remove any imported history from Settings.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
- Copies you exported, pictures you saved, and the other devices you paired keep their own data until you delete it there.
- To stop the app reading players: turn off Notification access for MuStat in Android settings.

## Children
MuStat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data (it collects no data from anyone).

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell users in the app.
